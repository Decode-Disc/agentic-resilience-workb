# Native task method comparison corpus

Frozen November 19, 2026. This corpus supports the [qualification protocol](native-task-skills-qualification-protocol.md); it is not execution authority or a quality result.

The [case file](../../scripts/evaluations/workbench-browser/fixtures/native-task-methods-20261119/cases.json) contains the twelve analyst scenarios and separate assessment checks. The six historical requests, request dates and unavailable/undated cases are retained unchanged. The common Workbench corpus combines full historical readings recovered from retained Convex observations and verified against their original hashes; it does not recreate the historical search index. Source bytes and their provenance are frozen in these files. Only declared documents enter model observations, on demand. Assessment checks and [provenance](../../scripts/evaluations/workbench-browser/fixtures/native-task-methods-20261119/provenance.json) are reviewer material.

The two Adobe cases are held out from authored teaching examples, not from every previous project experiment. The September analysis discusses older financial results and broader competitive arguments; the June report describes assistant capabilities. The reviewer must judge actual relevance and permit a defensible source choice, rather than require a particular article solely because of its date. Neither article establishes transfer of final creative approval authority.

The four `sources-*.json` files use the same real CCC observations. One intentionally truncates the reinspection release before the paragraph describing insurer-provided rules. Another presents two issuer surfaces of the same announcement alongside independent reporting about financing. The latter is independent reporting, but is not independent verification of estimate-review performance. The scope case contrasts 2024 product claims, 2026 quarterly results and forecasts about 2027 financing. Complete acquisition means the full retained normalized representation is supplied; original publisher completeness was not independently verified. These boundaries remain material to assessment.

The interruption case pauses genuinely started research after the first retained capture and asks Workbench to explain retained material and unfinished work without resuming. Its unavailable customer-check URL is explicitly synthetic; it is never fetched. The particular captures obtained before pause depend on the model. Failure to retain useful material, absence of unfinished work, or failure to pause cannot count as a passed interruption case. A failed acquisition alone is insufficient.

The campaign preparer checks each Sources file's byte hash, parses its documentary fields, then binds the canonical content hash into the immutable mandate. A changed file or inline content hash is refused. Both arms use these identical inputs and current date handling; only retained inline methods versus native skill catalogue consultation differ. No label, expected conclusion or assessment rationale is inserted into documentary text.
