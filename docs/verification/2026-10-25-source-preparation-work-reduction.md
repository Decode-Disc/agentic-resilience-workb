# Source preparation work reduction

Date: 2026-10-25. Target: the existing local backend `local-aboul_panga-dd_proto` at `http://127.0.0.1:3210`. Source concurrency remains two. This implementation and its verification made no paid provider calls and did not resume either real ServiceNow dossier.

## Implemented behavior

New preparations finish permitted supplemental collection and qualification before the final comparison pass. Documentary gaps for supplementation share the existing source classifier's company, purpose, period, authority and independence checks, while remaining separate from final use restrictions. A pending comparison alone does not imply a missing document; rejected sources cannot satisfy the planning projection. Existing preparations without the execution snapshot retain their durable step order.

New captures pin their qualification-input policy. If all available structured passages and the response schema fit the 128 KiB allowance, qualification sends them without creating an index or searching passages first. Otherwise it retains retrieved selection. Captures remain partial when their reading was partial; whole available input is not evidence of complete access to the original document. Historical captures retain their previous input policy and unknown provider outcomes remain blocked. Selection strategy is included in request identity and retained provenance. Indexing remains available on demand for comparison and approved saved-source search.

Native search reads prefetch two bounded queries and consume their results in the original order. Each query still searches at most two captures with 64 results per capture. Both sibling reads settle before a failure propagates, with no new search window launched after that failure. Ranking and evidence references are unchanged.
