## Analyst intent clarification — 2026-11-18

The analyst confirmed after the lot that “will it survive after the recent crisis ?” referred to the governance crisis surrounding Jensen Huang's departure. They deliberately left the event unnamed to see whether Workbench would find it. NVIDIA's [November 12 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm) reports that its board accepted Huang's resignation on November 11. The filing was available when the replay ran on November 18.

The [retained workflow evidence](../../scripts/evaluations/archives/20261118T111109244Z-campaign-nvidia-grounded-framing-relay-fix-20261118-lot-1/record.json) records the agent's lookup query as `NVIDIA Corporation recent crisis event 2024 2025 export controls market selloff China revenue`. The application forwarded that query without a publication-date filter. The eight references delivered to the agent dated from February to May 2025. The trace establishes that the search was already directed at an older topic before the H20 program was written. It does not establish what the same provider would have returned for a different query. The recorded verdict and the offline framing-write regressions retain their original scope.

For future qualification, retain the original analyst message and the November 18 context in a discovery case. Check whether Workbench finds and reads contemporary support for the event, then proposes an economically relevant program grounded in it. Use a separate clarification case whose relevant evidence still leaves multiple interpretations that would change the research. Keep the analyst's later confirmation and the reference filing in the assessment evidence for the discovery case. These criteria have not been exercised in a new live run.
