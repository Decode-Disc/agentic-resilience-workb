### Provisional saving invalidates an unread candidate

In the retained-control independent-scrutiny case, a search succeeded, the model read the product and acquisition announcements, and a framing write created revision 2. A subsequent attempt to read the financial-results candidate from that same search returned `source_reference_not_observed`. Retained search/read records match on run, mission, message and instruction version; their framing revisions differ. The search was completed and adopted without refusal. `requirePublicContextRequest` in `convex/workbenchPublicContext.ts` requires the search revision to equal the current framing revision. This localizes the failure to the revision fence through retained state and source inspection; there is no separate branch-level telemetry or new executable reproduction in this evaluation.

The model consulted the refusal, called it transient, and retried with a corrupted search ID. That operation was rejected before dispatch. The mission reached `repetition_stop`, retaining its program but publishing no final reply. The final generation succeeded at the provider; this was not a provider outage or the earlier native publication-continuation failure.

The bounded next correction belongs at candidate-reference authority and feedback: preserve access to an already authorized same-message candidate across a provisional save where the authority contract permits, or provide an explicit stale-reference outcome and supported refresh path. Preserve cross-run, cross-message and cancellation fences. Verify search → save → read an unread candidate → recover through the real operation boundary, including a settled partial outcome when reading cannot proceed.
