# Native generation recovery and documentary interruption — 2026-11-17

## Scope and result

The shared Pi transport now submits confirmed original tool arguments to canonical operation validation for structured definitions as well as Workbench. Invalid arguments produce a refused operation and native continuation; SDK repairs cannot execute the operation. Missing originals, incomplete replies, response capacity, authority and uncertain-effect protections remain enforced. The shared non-progress mechanism bounds repeated refusals without a separate retry engine or renewed allowance.

Sources missions blocked by unusable output or repeated non-progress suspend framed research rather than notify a waiting owner as a completed result. The retained mission supplies the explanation used by Workbench facts, research observations and the dossier projection. Resume preserves prepared material, consumption and the original deadline. Unclassified failures cannot be described as time exhaustion while the preparation window is still open. Existing analyst settlement and actual deadline/budget exhaustion regressions remain green.

Current contracts are maintained in [PRODUCT](../../PRODUCT.md#source-preparation-and-corpus-review) and [TECH](../../context/TECH.md#completion-and-publication). Historical Workbench-only malformed-argument qualifications remain dated evidence; their applicability is extended to structured native definitions by this correction, without rewriting their observations. No competing normative document was introduced.
