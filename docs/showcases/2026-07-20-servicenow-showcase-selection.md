## Candidate Decision

ServiceNow is a technically strong candidate for the primary completed and carefully evaluated showcase dossier. The selection is not final because the original candidature discussion advised avoiding companies Raphaëlle has already analyzed extensively. Audience originality must be reviewed before corpus curation begins.

This selects the company to research; it does not predetermine any dimension position, confidence, direction, claim, architecture relationship, or analyst conclusion. The showcase company remains configuration or fixture data and must not be hard-coded into domain or workflow logic.

## Selection Criteria

ServiceNow passed the following showcase gates during the design research:

- it is a US-listed technology company with resolvable SEC and investor-relations records;
- authoritative filings and substantial public product, platform, integration, security, and model documentation are available;
- the public record can inform all ten assessment dimensions while still leaving meaningful unknowns;
- its workflow and system-of-action position can support a useful architecture map without claiming complete knowledge of its internal architecture;
- its platform claims, federated-data behavior, integration boundaries, and model-provider options create genuine tensions suitable for counter-evidence and analyst override;
- an initial corpus of approximately twelve to sixteen primarily HTML sources appears narrow enough for a bounded prototype run;
- a final audience-fit review must identify an angle that adds original technical judgment beyond Raphaëlle's existing ServiceNow analysis;
- the case is legible enough for a demonstration without reducing the result to an investment recommendation or aggregate score.

These are selection findings, not verified resilience findings. Every eventual claim must still pass the normal source-approval, provenance, classification, and analyst-review workflow.

ServiceNow should be rejected as the showcase if the resulting dossier would mainly restate Raphaëlle's existing analysis rather than reveal the author's own technical and economic judgment.

## Initial Research Frame

The dossier should test, rather than assume, the following questions:

1. Which operational context does ServiceNow persist directly, and which context remains federated from external systems?
2. Where does ServiceNow control durable workflow execution versus merely orchestrating external actions?
3. Which actions can AI agents execute through governed platform capabilities?
4. Is ServiceNow positioned as the primary orchestrator, one orchestrator among several, or an application surface above another control plane?
5. How dependent are agentic capabilities on external model and cloud providers, and what does provider choice actually mitigate?
6. Which data, schema, interaction, and workflow assets create defensibility, and which belong primarily to customers or connected systems?
7. What produces switching costs: data model, workflows, integrations, governance, custom applications, ecosystem, or commercial packaging?
8. How observable is pricing resilience when much enterprise pricing is not public?
9. Which documented feedback and learning loops improve the platform, and which claimed loops lack public evidence?
10. Which ServiceNow layers are exposed if models, horizontal agent platforms, or system-native copilots absorb orchestration or interface functions?

## Alternatives Considered

### UiPath

UiPath provides a particularly strong contradiction between automation-platform control and exposure to model and platform vendors. It remains a strong future evaluation fixture, but choosing it first risks collapsing the narrative into an insufficiently tested claim that agentic systems simply replace RPA.

### Atlassian

Atlassian offers a strong context-graph and workflow case through Jira, Confluence, Rovo, Automation, and Forge. Rapid product evolution makes date control more difficult, and the system-of-action position is less direct outside Jira and connected applications.

### Salesforce

Salesforce offers exceptionally broad evidence across CRM, Data Cloud, Flow, MuleSoft, Slack, and Agentforce. It was not selected first because the likely corpus breadth would test scope containment more than the core evidence method.

### Snowflake

Snowflake offers a valuable boundary case between data platform, agent orchestration, and external systems of action. It was not selected first because its system-of-action narrative is less immediately legible for the primary demonstration.
