# ServiceNow Audience-Fit Decision

## Status

Rejected as the primary candidature showcase

## Date

2026-07-21

## Decision

ServiceNow will not be the primary completed showcase dossier presented to Raphaëlle d'Ornano.

The earlier [ServiceNow showcase selection](./2026-07-20-servicenow-showcase-selection.md) remains a historical record of why ServiceNow passed the technical selection gates. This record closes its outstanding audience-fit review and supersedes only its provisional showcase status.

[CCC Intelligent Solutions](./2026-07-21-ccc-showcase-selection.md) replaces ServiceNow as the primary showcase candidate.

## Rationale

ServiceNow remains technically well suited to the workbench. Its public record can support a bounded corpus, all ten assessment dimensions, precise product and platform evidence, meaningful counter-evidence, and a legible architecture map.

It fails the more important candidature criterion: the primary dossier must make the author's original judgment visible to Raphaëlle. Raphaëlle's public work already uses ServiceNow as an example of enterprise-software resilience and examines its possible transition from system of record to system of action. A ServiceNow dossier built around workflow control, context, orchestration, and abstraction risk would therefore overlap materially with analytical territory she has already made public.

The risk is not that the resulting dossier would be technically weak. The risk is that it would be interpreted mainly as an implementation or visualization of Raphaëlle's existing analysis rather than as evidence that the author can apply the underlying research problem independently to a new company.

## Public Audience-Fit Evidence

The audience-fit decision relies on the following public record:

- In [May by D'Ornano + Co.](https://www.decodingdiscontinuity.com/p/the-pe-buyout-opportunity-amid-techs-reckoning-cc3adec11d43), published in June 2024, Raphaëlle used ServiceNow as a benchmark for SaaS quality and resilience.
- In [The $285 Billion “SaaSpocalypse” Is the Wrong Panic](https://www.decodingdiscontinuity.com/p/285-billion-saaspocalypse-wrong-panic), published in February 2026, she explicitly included ServiceNow among systems of record with a credible path toward becoming systems of action through workflow context and proximity to intent.
- The [AGNT Orchestration Economics Manifesto](https://orchestration-economics.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-04-30-AGNT-Manifesto-PAGE.pdf), published in spring 2026, discusses ServiceNow within its software-sorting analysis and applies orchestration, workflow, API, and agentic-transition questions that substantially overlap the proposed dossier frame.

These sources establish meaningful public overlap. They do not establish the extent of Raphaëlle's private or institutional research, and this decision makes no claim about unpublished work.

## Why CCC Replaces ServiceNow

CCC preserves the qualities that made ServiceNow attractive while improving audience originality:

- it offers a multi-party workflow with visible questions about intent, context, action rights, coordination, and abstraction;
- insurance claims are directly relevant to Raphaëlle's public orchestration research, so the case remains immediately legible to her;
- CCC itself is not a recurring named case in the indexed public material reviewed for this decision;
- the photo-led auto physical damage workflow supports a falsifiable question rather than a predetermined resilience narrative;
- the case can demonstrate independent technical-economic judgment by testing whether CCC is a durable cross-party orchestration point or a replaceable specialist layer.

Absence from the reviewed public material is not evidence that CCC has never been analyzed privately. It is only a narrower and more defensible audience-originality basis than ServiceNow provides.

## Permitted Future Use of ServiceNow

ServiceNow may be retained as an internal calibration or evaluation case because Raphaëlle's public analysis provides a useful external reference against which to test whether the workbench surfaces relevant evidence and uncertainty.

That calibration work:

- must not delay the candidature-ready CCC dossier;
- must not be presented as the primary original contribution;
- must remain clearly labelled as calibration or evaluation material;
- must not encode Raphaëlle's conclusions as predetermined expected outputs;
- must preserve counter-evidence, unknowns, and independent analyst review.

ServiceNow is not required for the first credible prototype or the candidature demonstration. It should be added only if it closes a measured evaluation gap after the CCC vertical slice works.

## Consequences

- CCC is the only primary showcase candidate currently accepted for corpus curation.
- ServiceNow-specific release selection and corpus-curation work are no longer prerequisites.
- The provisional ServiceNow research questions may be reused only for later calibration and must not influence CCC's conclusions.
- No company name may be hard-coded into domain records, workflow logic, provider routing, or reusable UI behavior.
- This decision changes showcase prioritization, not the product invariants, technical architecture, assessment dimensions, or evidence rules.
