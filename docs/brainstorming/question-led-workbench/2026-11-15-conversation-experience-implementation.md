## Outcome and accepted direction

Make Workbench useful throughout the dossier: the analyst can read a progressively delivered response, understand actual work and its result, inspect relevant dossier context, refine the research program, and act on genuine prerequisites without coordinating internal agents. Preserve the economic question, evidence provenance and analyst authority across the conversation and dedicated research views.

- Deliver public response text progressively instead of waiting for the final message. Keep provisional text distinguishable from controlled publication, preserve interrupted partial text, and never expose hidden reasoning or internal diagnostics.
- Improve Markdown reading structure and spacing, with smooth arrival and reader-controlled following. Preserve exact message text and citations when rendering or copying.
- Explain conversational operations and independent missions through observed actions, outcomes, limitations and useful destinations. Apply the same execution semantics to Workbench, Sources and later connected actors, with domain-specific meaning supplied by their owners.
- Use a temporary compact work summary near the composer only when active work would otherwise become hard to find. Remove redundant or obsolete information; completed work remains in history and the relevant dossier view. Keep an indication beyond execution only for an unresolved consequence or available analyst action.
- Replace the floating context card with an escapable side panel when the conversation has sufficient width and a full-width panel on narrow layouts. Group sources and link to their existing complete collection instead of listing every source by default.
- Keep the proposed research program compact by default. Review reveals one complete question, one selector available on demand and an optional rationale. Preserve optional editing and addition, and the explicit Start research decision without requiring every question to be visited. Once launched, remove the preparation panel from the composer and retain the accepted program in dossier context.

The illustrative interaction studies establish direction, not executable workflow, exact dimensions or additional capacity. In particular, a study with five questions does not override the server-owned question allowance.

## System brief

**System type** — Complicated for execution and projections: code establishes the provider buffering, final publication boundary and disconnected Sources operation list. Complex for analyst comprehension: a smaller panel can still hide a necessary decision, and screenshots cannot establish ease of use. No incident evidence requires a chaotic-system stabilization response.

**Players and incentives**

For software actors, the last column describes enforced acceptance conditions and misleading proxies, not inferred human motives.

| Actor | Useful outcome | Acceptance condition or proxy to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analyst | Understand the economic question, inspect support and retain authority with little coordination. | A clear next action and preserved decisions; reading every question or expanding every operation is not a success metric. |
| Workbench | Explain available dossier material and coordinate permitted requests. | Recorded effects and versioned outcomes constrain explanations; fluency or response completion does not prove that work succeeded. |
| Sources | Prepare useful, traceable documentary material and explicit gaps. | Controlled contributions and need outcomes, rather than reference counts or apparent visual activity. |
| Investigation, target capability | Prepare reasoned appraisals using controlled assertions and question-specific uses. | Controlled substantive results or justified insufficiency; a completed source mission cannot satisfy these conditions. |
| Synthesis, target capability | Connect retained appraisals into a supported mechanism and note, or a justified alternative finding. | Eligible inputs and joint controls; there is no reward for forcing a diagram or treating availability as adoption. |
| Convex, Pi Durable and presentation | Preserve authority, durable continuity and consistent observable state. | Ordered, attributable observations and controlled effects; animation and local component state cannot manufacture progress. |
| Administrator and prototype author | Inspect failures and deliver a credible demonstration within authorized resources. | Protected diagnostics and measured limits; call counts, tokens, visual polish or paid reruns do not establish analytical usefulness. |

**Stocks and flows** — Messages, native commits, operation receipts, evidence versions, decisions and unfinished work accumulate. Authorized execution changes their states; public projections determine what the analyst can understand. A growing transcript increases retrieval effort, while additional streaming commits consume real storage and backend work. No current measurement establishes a numerical throughput or capacity problem; collect measurements before changing policy.

**What the change sets off** — Progressive feedback can reduce uncertainty and repeated submissions, a dampening loop to test rather than claim. More partial commits can increase storage and write pressure, delay settlement and trigger premature stops, a reinforcing failure loop. Permanent activity summaries accumulate visual noise and conceal actionable exceptions, another reinforcing loop. Shared typed projections dampen label and status divergence across surfaces. Delayed feedback matters: a reassuring draft can precede a refused or corrected final explanation, so provisional text cannot become evidence, a saved decision or a completed mission.

**Protocol** — Trace and test the technical contracts at their real integration boundaries; then run small reversible interface trials around realistic concurrent states. Keep one state owner per concept and use existing native mechanisms before adding infrastructure.

**Next smallest test** — Extend the existing unpaid native integration fixture with an externally controlled stream that remains open after its first public text. Demonstrate a partial native observation before EOF, zero premature business effects or final publication, then a single authorized final publication. Failure to obtain that observation through the native path falsifies the assumed streaming seam and requires revising the dependent implementation tasks before building a second store.

## Architectural choice and consistency contract

Fact: Pi already owns partial generation state; Convex owns authorization and public publication; assistant-ui already supports running messages and smooth Markdown. The missing path is guarded progress through native persistence and a safe reactive public projection.

Options: expose a filtered native view through the existing Convex bridge, or maintain a bounded derived public snapshot when serving that view directly would require excessive replay. Both are viable; a derived snapshot must identify its source commit and invocation and remain disposable. A separate authoritative streaming transcript or independent transport service would duplicate state and is not required.

Recommendation: first prove native progress through the existing bridge, then select the smallest bounded reactive read model from its measured behavior. Fix the exact projection shape and transaction attachment point in the first implementation slice, before its consumer is written. Do not expose Pi documents, model reasoning, tool arguments, raw results or administrator journals to the browser. Only explicitly analyst-facing prose is eligible: Sources structured completions, documentary checks, Investigation/Synthesis structured artifacts and auxiliary generations are not conversational text. Their useful progress comes from domain-owned observations. Do not replay an entire native log on every subscription update.

Keep these distinctions across every affected surface:

| State or event | What it establishes | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Queued or steered analyst message | Delivery intent and admitted instruction state. | Completed handling or a research pause. |
| Provisional response text | Public text generated so far by one identified attempt. | Published answer, successful operation or analytical artifact. |
| Completed conversation turn | Workbench finished responding under publication controls. | Sources, Investigation or Synthesis completion. |
| Mission completion | That mission handed back its controlled outcome. | Complete evidence, successful every-need preparation or analyst adoption. |
| Request transmission | A responsible mission received an authorized instruction. | Treatment, satisfaction or a completed documentary complement. |
| Capture, contribution, assertion, appraisal, retained decision | The exact artifact and version represented by its domain owner. | Any stronger stage inferred from a count or conversation text. |
| Stop response, Pause research, Resume research | Their existing separate scopes and prerequisites. | Authority to restart another scope, bypass review or enlarge the resource grant. |

### 1. Establish the streaming boundary without changing publication

Owners: `convex/integrations/piMission.ts`, `piStorage.ts`, `convex/execution/piStore.ts`, `conversations.ts`, `workbench.ts`, and `execution/schema.ts` only if the selected derived projection needs optional persisted fields.

Confirm the new test fails because the public partial is absent before EOF, rather than because imports, setup or admission fail.

Forward normalized nonterminal progress through the guarded provider and native partial mechanism while preserving existing before-dispatch, receipt, operation and adoption checks. Executable tool results and terminal completion must not escape these checks merely because text is streaming. Prove that the native throttle settles its pending write before termination and that rejected or stale outputs cannot later become a live reply.

The public projection must carry the active turn/mission, invocation attempt, native sequence, current instruction version and public text with its presentation phase. Bind workflow, generation and corpus/version authority where the existing execution requires them. Recheck authority before retaining progress and before exposing it. Reject out-of-order or superseded updates, exclude auxiliary compaction and control generations, and use monotonic identity rather than append-only browser guesses. Keep large text bounded or paged under the existing policy rather than introducing an arbitrary truncation that silently loses a reply.

Cover interrupted partial retention, Stop without research pause, steering during generation, direct framing changes, correction superseding the provisional candidate, duplicate receipts, stale writers and reopen/reconnect without another provider call. History must contain one final message; interrupted unpublished text remains explicitly partial and cannot be confused with that final message. Preserve controller-disabled, controller-unavailable and residual-reserve cases.

Measure first observed public text relative to EOF, native/public commit counts and bytes, write latency, retained session growth and settlement delay on the same fixed realistic transcript before and after. Use deterministic externally gated streaming and actual native serialization/storage, not a spy-count claim or fake-time benchmark. Retain existing provider accounting, deadlines, leases, capacity and unknown-outcome behavior. Reuse native progress settings first; change cadence only if measurements justify it and keep any legitimate tunable in its owning policy. No inference retry, new service, provider switch or dependency is part of this slice.

Exit: an authorized subscriber can observe provisional text before terminal generation; final publication, replay and all refusal paths remain correct; the read-model contract and measured persistence cost are recorded. This gate blocks the connected streaming UI, not independent typography or program work.
