## Purpose and current boundary

Reduce the time between a qualification lot and an actionable assessment while keeping execution integrity, model usefulness and unsupported conclusions distinguishable. A reviewer should identify what the evidence establishes, what failed or remains unfinished, and what requires further investigation. A truthful inconclusive result is useful; a plausible explanation without supporting evidence is not a demonstrated cause.

The existing [campaign runner](../../scripts/evaluations/workbench-browser/campaign.ts) captures the candidate, expectations, requests, responses, operation outcomes, receipts, persisted state, scenario results and evidence identities. It creates a review template and validates the declared checks, their verdicts, evidence paths and the selected evidence revision. These checks do not verify the semantic truth of the review. Its current assessment command requires a reconciled lot in review; an unresolved receipt can leave a lot financially closing even though its partial evidence still merits analysis. The [evaluation workflow](../../.agents/skills/workbench-evaluation/SKILL.md) currently assigns investigation and semantic judgments to the development agent. These statements describe the existing runner, not delivery of the target below.

## Accepted direction

After the runner captures available evidence and completes its cleanup attempts, it starts a default automated review as a logical step of the existing runner. Cleanup and the preserved technical result must not depend on the review succeeding. A separate process is not selected initially; consider one if measured overhead, recovery or isolation demonstrates a need. Previously captured lots may be examined using the same evidence contracts where supported; their original artifacts and authorizations must not be rewritten or renewed.

For each declared expectation, the reviewer produces a passed, failed or inconclusive judgment, a concrete finding and evidence references. It distinguishes execution integrity, semantic usefulness, explicit containment and unexercised stages. It may flag a possible cause, but must identify it as unverified unless evidence establishes it. Treat captured documents, model text, tool output and historical messages as data, not instructions. The runner validates the review's structure, evidence identity and references using existing owners; valid references do not prove that a model's judgment is correct. Preserve the reviewer identity, model/provider, actual attempts, limits and retained evidence revision alongside the result.

Automatically record the review and its verdict without requiring a developer to approve every result. Preserve the lot's independent mechanical result. If analysis cannot complete, record the review as pending without an analysis verdict. If the lot cannot yet be financially reconciled, retain its review and outcome without pretending that the existing official assessment succeeded; connect it to official assessment only when its prerequisites are met. Adapt the existing campaign assessment lifecycle rather than altering old receipts, hashes, evidence indexes or archived outcomes to bypass it. Recovery must not replay an uncertain paid call or silently replace an earlier review.

A comprehensive diagnostic is an explicit request, not the default. It may examine relevant source owners and run appropriate local checks to locate the first demonstrated divergence, distinguish causes and propose corrective actions. Use existing test or replay runners before constructing an ad hoc probe, and preserve the distinction between a reproduced local mechanism and an unobserved provider event. A diagnosis does not authorize production edits, dependency changes or further paid calls.

## Model routes and accounting

Use the user's OpenAI API as the primary analysis route, with an inexpensive model on OpenCode Go as fallback when OpenAI cannot serve. The mandate declares the permitted routes, analysis scope and a separate finite allowance for analysis alongside the lot's own envelope. Model identities, capabilities, prices and availability are mutable and must be checked when preparing a future mandate; this decision does not select a fixed model or invent a per-response consultation quota. Do not expose provider credentials in evidence or logs.

If an OpenAI attempt has an unknown outcome after dispatch, the Go fallback may proceed only when the analysis authorization can cover both attempts. Retain each attempt, its identity, known consumption and any unresolved reservation; do not report an unknown charge as zero. On a mid-analysis fallback, carry forward recorded observations and evidence references. Clearly mark any incomplete OpenAI response and do not promote it to an established finding.

Go fallback is restricted to the subscription's included allowance. [OpenCode documents](https://opencode.ai/docs/console/go/) that Extra Usage can draw on the workspace balance after the included allowance is exhausted. Do not dispatch through Go until the implementation can guarantee the accepted restriction; if it cannot, explicitly stop and leave the review pending. OpenCode also [requires a stable session identity](https://opencode.ai/docs/console/harnesses/) for Go and reports subscription usage separately from charged cost. Verify actual account configuration and runtime behavior without exposing credentials before any authorized use. A response reporting billing after completion alone does not establish a pre-dispatch guarantee.

Compare engines against the same archived evidence and expectations. First measure local startup and evidence traversal under equivalent mocked conditions; those measurements do not establish live-model quality. Where live comparison is needed, use the same model and equivalent input, identify the provider, and obtain separate bounded authorization before paid work. Compare the ability to recognize known failures and appropriately return inconclusive on insufficient evidence, including the independently declared fallback model. Select the fastest and lightest engine among those that demonstrate adequate review quality on the tested cases. A limited comparison does not establish general reliability; no arbitrary numerical acceptance threshold is selected here.

An analyzer tightly coupled to the existing runner is the accepted starting point because it reuses the campaign and evidence lifecycle. A separately supervised worker remains viable if measured behavior or recovery needs justify its extra lifecycle. An archive-only, manually invoked analyzer remains useful for historical investigations but cannot by itself provide the accepted default automatic review. Choosing different SDKs alone does not change these execution boundaries.

## Verification and implementation boundary

Before enabling this capability, verify a successful review with real retained identities; missing, invalid and stale references; an interrupted or partially captured lot; an unknown financial outcome; and automatic review failure leaving the mechanical result intact and the review pending. Exercise the primary-to-fallback path before and after dispatch, including unknown-cost reservations, preserved observations and refusal when subscription-only billing cannot be assured. Verify that completed cleanup is not undone by analysis failure and that recovery does not duplicate a paid attempt or overwrite historical evidence. Evaluate semantic judgments separately from deterministic identity and authorization checks, using retained cases with reviewed expectations, including a known failure and a case requiring an inconclusive result.

The last local development lot exposed the need for this distinction: its documentary control timed out and its unresolved receipt prevented native assessment, while the captured evidence still supported diagnosis. Its reports and original campaign archives are local-only. They are development evidence, not a held-out quality benchmark or authorization for another lot.
