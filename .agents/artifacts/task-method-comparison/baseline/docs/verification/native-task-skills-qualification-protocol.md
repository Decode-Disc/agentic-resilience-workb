# Native task skills qualification protocol

Prepared November 19, 2026. Status: proposed future paid mandate; no execution or cost authorization. Technical behavior and semantic usefulness are separate judgments. Current execution contracts belong in [TECH](../../context/TECH.md#native-task-skills-and-dated-observations).

## Comparison

Compare a control retaining the previous methods with a treatment consulting native skills on demand. Both arms must receive identical corrected documentary observations, dates and conflicts, the same effective tools including the admitted native reader, model revision, effort, provider capabilities, coverage priority, initial dossier state and resource allowances. Only method instructions and catalogue use may differ. Do not compare an older date path to the corrected treatment.

Freeze the code revision, definition versions, package ID and hash, resource hashes, input corpus digest, model revision and capabilities, coverage preset, initial state and canonical resource-policy values before dispatch. Assert equality of observations and effective tool schemas outside the intentional method difference. Keep comparison bindings in evaluation fixtures; introduce no production strategy flag.

The existing evaluator selects registered definition versions. It does not yet provide a paired control binding retaining historical methods while admitting the same new reader. Historical Workbench 6 and Sources 4 do not provide capability parity by themselves. Implement and deterministically validate that bounded evaluation binding before requesting execution. No fallback live comparison is authorized.

## Fixed cases

Use 12 cases per arm, 24 executions total. Keep the six historical Workbench cases and their original analyst messages, dates and adverse fixtures: NVIDIA H20 supplied filing; CCC Balanced; CCC First-party depth; CCC Independent scrutiny; lookup unavailable; insufficient dated support. Their previous results remain historical evidence.

Add two held-out Workbench cases independent of the authored teaching examples: a newer publication describing an older unrelated event alongside a relevant earlier release; an analyst mentioning an announcement without asserting a change in workflow authority. Freeze their captures and expected distinctions before dispatch.

Add four launched Sources cases: a partial reading whose unread section could qualify a claim; syndicated issuer statements alongside genuinely independent reporting; useful counter-evidence with different period or scope; interrupted preparation retaining useful material and an unfinished check. These must actually launch preparation through the existing authorized fixture path. Pre-Start framing does not qualify Sources.

Teaching cases under `agent-skills/` are excluded from held-out acceptance. Freeze all 12 case identities, observations, adverse outcomes and expected human checkpoints in the future campaign manifest.

## Judgments

Assess Workbench request fidelity, justification of event relevance, containment of unsupported hypotheses and usefulness of the proposed program. Assess Sources contributions to specific needs, attribution and independence, limitations, confrontation and honest partial outcomes. A successful operation or a valid provenance identifier does not establish semantic success.

Report execution integrity, complete semantic success and explicitly contained partial outcomes separately, per case and arm. Reader counts, tokens and document counts are diagnostic measures. A material authority or provenance failure blocks acceptance of the affected capability. Accurate stylistic imperfections do not authorize tuning or another batch.

Use a fresh reviewer against the frozen analyst request, original observations, saved artifacts and full operation outcomes. Record every finding and disagreement; do not substitute implementer judgment for a completed independent assessment.

## Future execution gate

Before seeking paid authority, supply the completed parity check, exact model/provider/effort choices, each arm's effective canonical resource envelope, maximum workflow and reviewer cost in dollars, reviewer procedure and failure/recovery accounting. Cost ceilings are intentionally unapproved here; dispatch remains prohibited until the user grants explicit numeric ceilings covering all 24 executions and review. The former six-case authorization cannot fund this comparison.

A single frozen batch is the proposed unit of judgment. Preserve both arms' traces and costs, including failed attempts. Any follow-up requires its own bounded rationale and authority. This protocol makes no claim that skills improve economic judgment.
