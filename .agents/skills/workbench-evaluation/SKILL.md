# Workbench Evaluation

Help decide which approach produces useful evidence or a defensible analytical result at an acceptable cost. Keep the analyst's actual question, inspectable reasoning and ability to continue the workflow ahead of benchmark scores. Use existing evaluation runners and the archive command within their supported scope rather than building another evaluation framework.

## When to Use

Use when qualifying the agent harness or a model-backed application path, comparing model candidates, retrieval methods, source-selection policies or analytical approaches, interpreting their recorded results, or diagnosing where useful evidence or reasoning failed. This includes planning evaluation of appraisals and synthesis without assuming those stages or their runners are implemented. The skill can be selected automatically for those tasks or invoked as `$workbench-evaluation`.

## When Not to Use

Standalone unit tests, visual-only UI checks, security audits and performance investigations use their dedicated workflows. For an authorized model-backed browser qualification, use the browser workflow to exercise the application and this skill to judge the outcomes.

Respect the requested mode. Inspection and planning remain read-only: propose the selection or use the archive preview instead of writing a snapshot. An authorized evaluation can retain its results within its agreed scope.

## Choose the Required Investigation

An unclear result does not always require another experiment. Select the smallest investigation that can answer the user's question so existing evidence is used before new measurements consume time or money. Identify the requested outcome, inspect the relevant implementation and saved artifacts, then choose the appropriate path:

| Request | Procedure and stopping point |
| --- | --- |
| Interpret recorded results | Reconstruct the conditions and explain what the saved observations establish. Stop with a supported interpretation and visible unknowns; do not launch a new comparison merely because the record is incomplete. |
| Diagnose lost evidence | Follow the affected source or passage through the saved capture, selection, prepared input and accessible downstream material. Locate the first demonstrated loss, or identify the missing observation that prevents localization. Keep the diagnosis narrow unless evidence justifies expanding it. |
| Examine an analytical result | Trace the disputed assertion, question-specific use, appraisal or synthesis relationship to its inputs, reasoning and controls. Distinguish a reasoning error from missing evidence, an unfinished check or a pending human decision. |
| Qualify the harness or application path | Separate execution integrity from answer quality. Trace the request, operation result, persisted state, published reply and continuation; identify which of those boundaries the exercised path actually covers. |
| Compare approaches | Define the decision and common conditions, then prepare the smallest informative comparison. Execute only within its authorization and stop at the agreed decision or evidence boundary. |

If the goal is ambiguous, inspect the product context and existing results first, then ask one focused question about the unresolved decision. Do not turn a request for better analyst results into a model benchmark by default. Interpretations and diagnoses use the relevant framing and interpretation steps below; preparation and execution apply only when a new measurement is needed and authorized.

## Prepare and Execute

For a new qualification, use the existing campaign path when it covers the required boundary. Prepare a version-3 mandate for automated evidence review; version-1/2 mandates retain manual assessment without migration. Configuration, execution, evidence and cleanup stay under their existing owners. Recorded-result interpretation and read-only diagnosis do not require a campaign.

For correction loops, prefer a targeted campaign with `executionSurface: "backend"` when the uncertainty concerns model behavior, operations or persisted state. Select only the relevant path; avoid discovery and acquisition when they are not the subject of the measurement. Backend execution uses the same production agents and public operations without the UI and stops at the first failed scenario. Select `executionSurface: "browser"` explicitly to qualify the stabilized candidate for rendering, subscriptions, navigation and reconnection; backend success does not supply that evidence. Use the existing offline runners when retained inputs alone can resolve the question. No elapsed-time improvement is established until the chosen paths are measured.

The runner owns temporary configuration, candidate freezing, preflight checks, scenario execution, evidence capture, accounting, cleanup and its native archives. In version 3, Pi independently investigates the captured run and archived source using the shared [reviewer mission](references/reviewer-mission.md); the runner publishes its developer handoff independently of assessment acceptance. The development agent prepares the decision and mandate, verifies decisive findings and targeted hypotheses rather than reconstructing the entire run, performs manual assessment where needed and makes only authorized corrections. The runner submits the validated assessment only after reconciliation. The user decides material trade-offs and grants or expands the mandate; do not ask them to score each lot or reconfirm work already covered by it. Runner validation of evidence identities does not prove the reviewer's judgment is true.

Predicting an agent's required consultations can exhaust an artificial allowance before its answer or recovery is observable. Use the agreed cumulative resource envelope for adaptive work; do not invent per-agent, per-scenario or per-response call shares to reserve coverage for later cases. Scenario caps belong only to an explicitly requested boundary test or an agreed scenario-specific resource constraint. Count paid generations, controls and tool operations separately: one generation can request several tools, and consultation, correction and final publication can require further generations. Before authorization, inspect the resolved manifest for unintended sublimits. If ordering protects a pending observation, preserve every required case and expectation and disclose the changed order; it does not guarantee complete coverage within the envelope. Unrun or budget-truncated stages remain unqualified.

1. **Select the measurement.** State the decision, boundary and success criteria using [Frame the Decision](#frame-the-decision). Before proposing a budget or mandate, state the target operation to observe, where the available runner can actually start, which paid stages it must traverse before reaching that operation, and the decisive trace that will prove the target operation was reached. Identify any direct starting fixture or entry point on captured material; when only an earlier entry point is available, disclose the risk of exhausting the allowance before reaching the target operation unless traversing the full handoff from that starting point is itself the goal. Start with offline preparation, replay or deterministic mode when that resolves the uncertainty; simulation does not establish model quality. Inspect current runner support and retain comparable inputs, reference judgments and code identities. The [source-selection instructions](../../../scripts/evaluations/source-selection/README.md) govern documentary experiments. If no runner supports the boundary, identify the gap instead of silently building another harness.
2. **Prepare the campaign.** Read the [campaign command and manifest reference](references/campaigns.md) when creating a mandate or selecting configurations. Use the schema and existing flags rather than editing scripts for each company, path, model or budget. Verify workflow catalogue readiness and reviewer provider/model availability through Pi. Refresh official availability and pricing for API-paid routes; use the reference's native subscription accounting path where applicable. Retain the resolved mandate through `--campaign prepare` for review; preparation grants no execution authority. Keep reference labels and answer keys out of provider inputs.
3. **Authorize once within bounds.** Before external inference or retrieval, including subscription inference, obtain explicit approval for the exact lot or immutable bounded campaign under the [technical qualification contract](../../../context/TECH.md#access-resources-and-security), then record it with `--campaign authorize`. The mandate identifies workflow routes, efforts, scenarios, correction scope, per-lot and cumulative limits, number of lots and expiration. Automated review requires its own explicit routes and analysis allowances, separate from workflow spending. A skill invocation or historical approval does not authorize a new campaign.
4. **Run one lot.** Use `--campaign run` on the prepared output directory and let the runner manage its supported lifecycle. Run deterministic regressions and relevant runtime checks before a paid qualification of the final candidate. Do not reproduce the runner's configuration, capture or cleanup steps in ad hoc scripts. After interruption, inspect `--campaign status` and the retained artifacts before recovery. Cleanup and reconciliation do not replay uncertain effects, but version-3 continuation may make further reviewer calls and requires remaining analysis authority. Follow the reference's recovery procedure; unknown external outcomes block blind retries.
5. **Assess the retained outcome.** For version 3, start with the developer report and its independent assessment state in the [artifact reference](references/campaigns.md#assessment-artifacts). Verify decisive citations and the proposed source boundary, then perform the smallest targeted check that can resolve a material uncertainty. Older completed JSON reviews remain inspectable without launching another reviewer. A useful handoff may remain advisory while assessment is invalid or reconciliation is blocked. For manual assessment, fill every declared check in `review-template.json` with a verdict, concrete finding and retained evidence paths, then use `--campaign assess`. Keep execution integrity, model quality and inconclusive or unexercised checks separate. If native assessment rejects evidence, preserve the artifacts and report the tooling boundary; do not rewrite hashes or indexes to force acceptance.
6. **Continue or close.** Resolve each lot's assessment before another. Continue without renewed permission when the active mandate covers the next configuration, declared repetition or identified correction; make corrections only within its authorized scope. A correction creates a distinct candidate whose required coverage must be established again. Stop at the agreed decision or resource boundary, retain the first failure and follow the reference's bounded closure procedure rather than treating `--campaign close` as an unconditional no-inference shutdown. A new route, battery, correction scope or larger allowance requires a new mandate; unused budget alone does not justify another lot. Apply [Required Evaluation Closeout](#required-evaluation-closeout) to failures and interruptions as well as successes.

For protected browser qualification, follow real admission, persisted operations, visible replies and reconnection on the target runtime. An isolated Convex experiment is evidence for its exercised boundary, not the deployed browser journey. Select relevant adversarial cases from observed failures: refused controls must preserve dialogue and documentary requests, concurrent edits must survive recovery, failed passage reads must permit recovery, and later exchanges must retain the native conversation. Verify the observable outcome, not merely the presence of a reply.

Measure calls, latency and charges by entry point. Explicit buttons and typed slash commands should be checked against their implemented deterministic contract, including zero model invocation for the control itself; resumed research may incur its own authorized work. Ordinary text can require model interpretation: do not propose keyword recognition to avoid that call. An unwired composer API cannot qualify a browser slash interaction. Keep provider latency separate from host waiting and application overhead when measurements permit attribution.

## Frame the Decision

A comparison is useful only if it answers the project's actual question. Inspect the analyst outcome, current implementation, available runners and saved evidence before proposing a new study.

Freeze captures, input preparation, reference judgments, code identities, model routes and parameters before dispatch. Record who authored the references and any unresolved judgments. Use equivalent inputs for common comparisons; evaluate real production preparation separately and do not pool different conditions into one ranking.

For time-sensitive questions, retain the original wording, dossier state and effective date. Establish what the evidence available at that date could reasonably identify. Evaluate discovery of a materially supported event separately from useful clarification when relevant evidence leaves several interpretations open. Keep the reference event out of the agent's input unless the analyst supplied it. If later clarification changes the reference judgment, date the revision and preserve the original inputs and verdict.

Name the evaluated boundary: framing, documentary preparation, assertion and use admission, appraisal reasoning or synthesis. For model-assisted work, identify the task's need for semantic interpretation or reasoning and the reliable deterministic alternative, where one exists. A comparison does not presume that every phase needs a model or that source acquisition must precede every model task. Early framing suggestions cannot establish company facts. Evaluation does not bypass research-start authority or activate an unavailable route.

State the question or decision, evidence boundary and success criteria before inspecting new outputs; identify alternatives when a comparison is needed. Reuse an existing protocol when it fits. Do not invent an acceptance threshold or treat a historical target, model, tariff or budget as permanent authority. Resolve material trade-offs with the user.

Freeze the resolved model configuration and reasoning choice independently for each agent role. Quality qualification uses the capability-aware recommendation from the configuration owner; a deliberately low or disabled reasoning setting must be identified as a stress condition. Medium is not an admission floor, and models without advertised levels must not receive invented settings. Verify instruction placement and the current business projection in the retained provider request before attributing a failure to the model. A manifest declaration alone does not prove the runtime used that model or effort.

For a robustness claim, include different companies, source families and dossier needs, including supplied references, targeted questions, uncertain arguments and counter-evidence. A narrow diagnosis may remain narrow if its conclusions say so. Keep development cases distinct from held-out checks and report prior exposure. Prefer useful imperfect sources with visible restrictions to unjustified exclusion.

Use small deterministic fixtures to isolate policy rules. For analytical regression, freeze the reference material, capture identities, cutoff and investigator-reviewed expected assertions, competing interpretations and decisive unknowns. Reuse the curated showcase where it fits rather than creating a competing golden dossier. Reference curation and human review of an answer key are evaluation preparation, not a universal application corpus-approval prerequisite. Offline evaluation may examine a target analytical task without certifying application delivery. When driving the application, preserve any enforced compatibility checkpoint until its replacement is implemented, verified and authorized. Preparation comparisons do not require several completed showcase dossiers.

Match semantic evaluation to the submitted object and intended use. Useful source preparation does not establish correct assertion use, a defensible appraisal or supported synthesis relationships. For those outputs, inspect the argument and its objections, unknowns, permitted evidence and revisions; check diagram-to-note coherence where applicable. Separate human adoption from automated control outcomes. Use the current product requirements to select relevant cases rather than inferring analytical quality from retrieval scores.

## OpenRouter Routing

By default, keep workflow qualifications representative of application routing: select the model and supported reasoning settings through the application catalogue and let OpenRouter select and load-balance upstream inference providers. Preserve the existing canonical `max_price` ceilings and account guardrails, and recall those protections when describing the proposed run. Specify an upstream provider when the user requests it or the evaluation has a demonstrated need, such as isolating a provider-specific failure or comparing endpoints. Explain that reason and the scope of the override in the configuration and authorization wording; keep the existing guardrails effective. A catalogue endpoint label or historical explicit pin alone does not justify an override. Record the provider actually used as diagnostic evidence and preserve historical execution identities. Keep the independent Pi reviewer's native provider and authentication selection separate from OpenRouter's upstream routing.

## Separate Harness Reliability from Model Quality

A weak model can expose defects that a stronger model avoids by chance. Judge the system's handling of an imperfect response separately from the response's economic usefulness, so model selection cannot hide a broken execution boundary.

| Dimension | Evidence to examine |
| --- | --- |
| Harness and application integrity | Authorization, actual state transitions, truthful published status, bounded completion, recovery after refusal or concurrent edits, retained messages and native conversation continuity. |
| Model task quality | Economic specificity, preservation of the requested meaning, useful clarification, evidence use, supported inference and readable analyst-facing language. |
| Exercised path | Deterministic fixture, real inference with isolated persistence, or protected browser through the actual application. Name the runtime and simulated boundaries; none automatically certifies another. |

Use the user's selected inexpensive stress model for repeated harness checks while mechanical defects remain. A poor analytical answer can coexist with a passing harness case; a convincing answer cannot excuse lost instructions, false status or a stuck response. Qualify the intended stronger candidate after the relevant harness corrections, unless the user requests a separate bounded model investigation. Keep stress and quality candidates explicit, verify their exact provider/model identities before dispatch, and never substitute a cheaper or similarly named model silently. These roles are evaluation choices, not permanent production defaults.

Before the final conclusion, investigate every material failure, including interrupted work, using the shared [first-pass diagnostic method](references/reviewer-mission.md#investigate-the-first-divergence). Start with Pi's handoff when available and extend it only for a material unresolved cause, missing observation or unsupported inference. Perform available in-scope read-only inspection and the cheapest safe targeted isolation check; do not defer accessible verification to another user prompt. Further investigation is the next action only when evidence, access or execution authority prevents completing it now.

A malformed reply followed by budget exhaustion does not establish failed recovery. Trace refusal, recorded feedback, continuation admission and actual provider dispatch separately. Identify the exhausted bound and its owner, including whether global authority remained when an evaluator-added sublimit stopped work. Feedback prepared in the harness is not proof the model received another request; a refused undispatched attempt leaves correction quality untested. Check existing recovery code and regressions before recommending a replacement mechanism. When a correction was dispatched, compare its actual returned arguments with the rejected ones: prose announcing a repair is not evidence that fields changed, and reusing an idempotent refusal is not a second executed effect.

For output-format failures, inspect the actual request before claiming structured output was enforced or unsupported. A schema in instructions, a tool-argument schema, JSON-only mode and provider-enforced JSON Schema are different constraints. Verify the selected transport and endpoint capabilities; distinguish an available but unused option from a proven incompatibility. Keep format/schema conformance separate from evidence validity and semantic correctness. An improvement opportunity is not automatically the cause of the observed stop or authorization to implement a transport change.

Attribute the observed divergence to the application harness, evaluation tooling, model, provider/runtime, a demonstrated interaction, or an unresolved cause. Explain the causal mechanism and cite the decisive artifact or reproducible check. Do not label an unsuccessful correction as a model weakness before checking its actual input; correct data in storage does not establish correct delivery. An invalid model call remains a model error, while unsafe refusal handling or a lost continuation can independently be a harness error. If evidence cannot separate the causes, name the missing observation and the cheapest discriminating check. A blocked state change does not make a contradictory published success claim acceptable. Do not diagnose a timeout from elapsed time alone.

Assess analyst attribution against the actual request and its granted scope. An authorized refinement may add explanatory wording; ambiguity alone does not prove invented analyst intent or justify another guard. Distinguish a useful elaboration from an unsupported fact, changed meaning or unauthorized action.

## Evaluate Hybrid Response Control

A controller can miss a contradiction, flag a correct explanation or prompt an unhelpful correction. Evaluate those stages separately so a favorable control score or successful publication cannot conceal an incorrect answer. Locate the current response-control contract in [TECH](../../../context/TECH.md) and inspect the policy actually pinned to each turn; model identities, thresholds and eligibility rules belong in their existing configuration owners, not this skill.

Trace the initial draft, frozen authoritative facts, filter outcome, controller assessment, corrective reminder, revised draft and published text with its separate notice. Judge initial and final answer quality independently of the controller. Use investigator-reviewed references to identify false alerts and missed contradictions; the controller's own score is not its answer key or a calibrated probability that the whole reply is true. Distinguish an incorrect answer visibly contained under the current publication contract from an unflagged false success claim. Neither is a semantic success, and valid advisory containment does not establish a mandatory execution failure.

When a comparison is needed, hold the generator, inputs and references constant while comparing eligible control-enabled and control-disabled cases. Include ambiguous but supported wording, genuine contradictions, unavailable checks and exempt replies. Count generator, controller and corrective calls separately, including latency, known charges and unresolved reservations. Check that ineligible replies produce no semantic-control call and that correction does not repeat business operations.

Keep development examples distinct from held-out cases. Threshold tuning creates a new policy version and requires independent validation; reusing exposed examples cannot establish general reliability. Preserve historical replacement cases as evidence for their original mechanism, and add current correction-and-reserve cases without rewriting their expected meaning.

## Define When to Stop

More measurements are useful only when they can change the decision. Before preparing a new batch, identify the observation that would justify choosing an approach, retaining the current one, or concluding that the available evidence is insufficient. This keeps the study tied to analyst usefulness rather than continued score improvement.

Use the existing success criteria and resource limits to define a bounded batch and its stopping condition. If no criterion is established, propose a concrete decision condition for the user's agreement rather than inventing a numerical target. A narrow diagnosis can stop at a demonstrated cause; it does not need a general winner.

Stop when the agreed question is answered, the authorized limit is reached, or another measurement cannot resolve the remaining uncertainty. If results are inconclusive, say so. Propose an additional batch only with the specific uncertainty it would resolve and the decision that could change. A small score difference alone does not justify expanding the study or replacing an adequate lower-cost approach.

## Keep Corrective Qualification Bounded

Changing scenarios between fixes hides coverage gaps and makes a successful run look more conclusive than it is. Correct a related set of failures at their shared owning boundary, then evaluate one frozen battery against explicit exit criteria. The aim is to decide whether the affected capability can advance, while keeping unrelated product work moving.

Start from the [versioned conversation regression cases](../../../scripts/evaluations/workbench/continuity-cases.json) when qualifying these boundaries. Record the battery hash, fixture provenance, model/route/effort, code fingerprints, runtime boundary and repetitions before dispatch. Preserve prior core cases when adding coverage. Label additional cases separately; a different state, forced compaction or browser transport is new coverage, not an interchangeable rerun. For conversation reliability, cross the relevant states with compaction instead of testing each only in isolation: unstarted work, active work and human prerequisites can fail differently.

Define separate outcomes for execution integrity, semantic task success and transparent containment. Compare original role-bearing messages, saved framing, operation receipts and current state with the actual published answer. A completed mission, safe refusal or code-owned partial reply is not a semantically successful answer. An unsupported status or authorship claim is material; an awkward but accurate formulation alone does not justify another correction cycle. Record whether a guard actually activated: model avoidance of a bad output does not qualify that guard; cite deterministic fault coverage separately.

Reproduce related defects deterministically through the public boundary before paid dispatch. Prefer corrections in the module that owns the shared contract. A prompt instruction is not deterministic enforcement. When a guard is deliberately limited to a closed vocabulary, disclose that boundary and keep the original output; do not claim that it verifies arbitrary paraphrases or reasoning.

Before writing an offline probe that imports production code, inspect the existing test and replay runners and choose the smallest one that supports the module’s actual imports and runtime. Smoke-test the real module import first. Use a custom loader only when the established tooling cannot exercise the required boundary; keep the production code unchanged and mock only external or nondeterministic boundaries. Treat import and setup errors as probe failures, not evidence of an application defect, and report what the probe actually establishes.

Run the unchanged core battery on the final candidate. Where authorized, repeat the same cases within the lot to expose variability; a single passing response does not establish absence of the defect, and a few repeats do not establish a general reliability rate. Retain a failed original and classify its first demonstrated divergence. Do not remove a failed case, change its expected meaning, substitute a model, or pool later corrections into the original result.

End the agreed batch with a capability-specific decision. Material state, authority, evidence or provenance failures block acceptance of the affected capability; record unresolved model limits and proceed with independent roadmap work where those limits do not apply. Do not start another paid batch merely because budget remains. An active campaign may cover declared repetitions or requalification of an identified, already authorized correction; otherwise another iteration needs separate authorization, a concrete unresolved decision and a bounded correction scope.

## Interpret the Results

Separate source inclusion, exact-span retention, equivalent useful evidence, quotation validity, factual support and downstream analytical correctness. A JSON-valid response is not necessarily correct. A missing expected span does not prove useful evidence is absent, and a favorable match count does not establish workflow completion.

For each apparent miss, inspect equivalent evidence and whether later processing can actually reach it. Record source inclusion, displayed qualification and downstream correctness separately. Observed captures and investigator-reviewed replays do not establish fresh-discovery or unfamiliar-company robustness; design a separate check before making those claims.

Report unavailable, not-run, invalid, wrong and inconclusive results separately. Show meaningful errors and investigator disagreements alongside successful cases. Keep estimates, provider-reported charges and unknown-charge reservations distinct. Compare useful quality, latency and available cost without assuming a winner. Preserve failed attempts and original scores; corrected references or changed inputs create a separate version rather than rewriting history.

Count attempted scenarios, completed responses, correct persisted effects and useful task outcomes separately. For each material case, compare the expected behavior with the observed state and published answer, then state the harness verdict, model-quality verdict and unresolved cause. Report real call count, elapsed time and reconciled charges for the identified code stage. A published answer is not necessarily a business success. A qualification conclusion must say whether the harness can continue to the intended model check, whether analytical usefulness remains insufficient, and which specific failures still prevent acceptance; a clean transport or schema result alone supports neither activation nor completion.

## Archive Retained Evidence

Results used in a conclusion need recoverable inputs. Before closing an experiment whose results are retained, identify its captures, protocols, references, catalogs, exact request manifests, response/error and cost journals, and actual relevant code snapshots. Include the report when present. Git HEAD alone does not identify uncommitted code, and current code cannot replace missing historical bytes. Resolve external replay inputs explicitly, including files under `/tmp`; a saved summary cannot replace them.

For a campaign, inspect its native archive record first. Use the archive command below for missing required inputs or supplementary retained evidence instead of recreating an archive the runner already verified.

Use `pnpm evaluation:archive --help` for the command's options, storage layout and recovery instructions. Preview the explicit selection with `--dry-run`, then run the same selection without that flag. Keep secrets and runtime configuration out. The command owns copying, hashing, packing, restoration verification and creation of the verified archive record; do not recreate those operations in the skill.

Link the report to the returned archive record. Report its verified status, included scope, missing inputs and local-only storage limitation. If an input is missing or verification fails, keep that failure visible and do not describe the experiment as fully archived or reproducible. Archiving proves byte preservation, not semantic correctness. Never remove originals as part of this workflow.

## Required Evaluation Closeout

The evaluation is not complete until the user receives a causal diagnosis and an actionable capability decision. Costs, scores, test counts, archive links and a statement that the run failed do not satisfy this requirement. Apply this gate to completed, interrupted and inconclusive lots; scale the detail to the material failures rather than auditing every harmless stylistic issue.

Before the final response, check that each material failure has all of the following:

- **Observed consequence and evidence:** what the analyst requested, what actually happened and the decisive request/output/state or executable check. Distinguish useful retained material, failed work and untested stages.
- **Attribution and certainty:** the first demonstrated failing boundary and its causal mechanism, classified under the categories above. Separate confirmed defects, supported inferences and unresolved causes; a controller score is not an answer key and a revised reply is not proof of repair.
- **Concrete next action:** the existing owner or boundary to correct, the smallest coherent correction, and the regression or observation that would establish resolution. If the cause is unresolved, specify an isolation step instead of a speculative fix. If no correction is justified, state why and what can be retained.
- **Capability decision:** what can proceed, what remains blocked or incomplete, and which next check could change that decision. Keep harness reliability, model task quality and advisory containment separate. A new measurement must answer a named uncertainty; unused budget does not justify another lot.

Put the material findings, their attribution and recommended actions in the user-facing answer itself; do not leave them only in a report or behind a link. State exactly what was checked and what remains unverified, then give the actual call count, known costs/unresolved reservations and retained evidence where applicable. Recommend the next bounded work without treating a recommendation as implementation or paid-execution authority. Preserve the original results when later diagnosis changes their interpretation; add a dated correction rather than rewriting raw observations or pooling code stages.

For successful cases, explain the demonstrated analyst benefit, relevant limits and why no further correction is needed. Use plain language before technical identifiers. The user should be able to act on the conclusion without asking a follow-up to discover whether the failure belongs to the harness or the model, or what must be done next.
