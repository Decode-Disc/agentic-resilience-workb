# Worked source preparation cases

## Partial capture and repeated issuer claims

Need: determine what a platform's routing assistant can execute without human approval. Observations: a retained product page shows recommendation generation but stops before its permissions section. Two news sites reproduce the issuer release word for word. An independent trade report includes a customer's statement that dispatch approval is still manual.

Selection: use the product passage for the advertised recommendation capability, with a partial-capture limit. Attribute the release's automation claim to the issuer; its syndicated copies do not independently verify execution. Read the customer report because its observed approval practice can confront the broader claim.

Prepared artifact: exact passages supporting advertised recommendations, an attributed issuer automation claim, and a separately attributed customer observation about manual approval. Restrict claims of universal autonomous execution. Preserve the different deployment scopes rather than declaring a contradiction across all customers.

Limits: the unread permissions section remains unfinished reading. Neither its absence from the capture nor one customer's configuration establishes a platform-wide impossibility.

## Interrupted preparation preserves a useful contrary observation

Need: identify dependency and switching constraints. Observations: a filing describes a long-term data agreement. A customer implementation guide describes an export path. Retrieval of the agreement's renewal terms is interrupted before a usable capture is retained.

Selection: qualify the filing's stated dependency and the guide's export capability separately. The export path is useful counter-evidence to an unrestricted lock-in claim, but says nothing about transition cost or data rights after termination.

Prepared artifact: two need-linked contributions with exact capture references, attributed terms and restricted uses. Record the renewal-term check as technically unfinished. Hand back the retained material with that limitation if authorized capacity cannot admit continuation.

Limits: do not report “no renewal restrictions found” or treat interrupted retrieval as an exhausted search. Investigation must assess the economic consequence; preparation has not decided resilience.
