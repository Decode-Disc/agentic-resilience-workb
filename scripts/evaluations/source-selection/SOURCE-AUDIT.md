# ServiceNow source-selection audit

Date: 2026-10-20. Dossier: `k979t5nzs6bmkxbp7eref2bkzx8bag4f`. Repository HEAD inspected: `531dbe1`. This is a read-only research audit, not a corpus approval, policy change, implementation, or assessment of ServiceNow's investment merits.

## Result

Useful independent material addressing the two missing counter-analysis requirements is already present among the alternatives. In particular, Hated Moats and the iFAST Research Team's FSM article discuss mechanisms that could weaken ServiceNow's seat economics, pricing, margins, or competitive position. Capital Blueprint also offers a relevant substitution argument, but contains a material numerical discrepancy. These are sources for investigating counterclaims, not validated counterclaims themselves.

The missing current, authoritative commercial-terms document remains a justified limitation. The Carahsoft PDF contains actual ServiceNow subscription terms, but its printed version is `12/5/2012`. The AWS listing supplies purchasing information rather than the subscription agreement. The Store applications concern customers' contract-management workflows rather than ServiceNow's own subscription contract.

The selector reproduces its policy exactly, but this dossier does not support calling the selection sufficiently robust. A newer, eligible investor presentation loses to an older recorded candidate, the older candidate now redirects to an events index, substantial SEC exhibits are labelled low information, and several exclusion explanations conceal a family-cap decision. More discovery alone would not resolve these defects.

## Selection robustness findings

### Hosting authority overrides document freshness

The comparator orders authority before provider result position, recency, research-angle matches, and stable source identity. The old presentation on the company domain outranks the newer presentation on a qualified shared IR host, then the investor-family cap excludes the newer one. Removing only the old presentation in memory makes the newer one recommended. This does not establish that every newer source is better, but demonstrates that a relevant recent eligible document is suppressed by a hosting distinction rather than an intentional period choice.

The selected old URL now returns an HTML events index, not the recorded PDF. A fresh retrieval confirms the final URL is `https://investor.servicenow.com/events-and-presentations/default.aspx`. This cannot prove that the same redirect existed when the proposal was created. It does show that stored source metadata and current document availability have diverged. No pre-approval document retrieval is proposed here: future design must distinguish permitted metadata validation from content validation after approval.

### Exclusion explanations collapse materially different situations

Creator Workflows, HR REST API, API Integration Demo, and the Q1 2026 presentation have distinct metadata contributions absent from the selected set, but hit family caps. They receive `no_new_coverage`. The Newsroom Q2 copy has the same contribution as an included release and can properly be omitted as repeated coverage. These situations require different explanations. They do not require indiscriminate inclusion of all five alternatives.

### SEC filing identity is not document identity

The policy classifies the two earnings exhibits as `regulatory_other`, because the stored sources lack structured exhibit type. Their actual headers identify EX-99.1. The policy has no adequate branch to classify such releases. It also treats accession identity as duplicate identity: the Q2 exhibit is marked as a duplicate of its parent 8-K even though these are separate documents. The included IR release limits harm in this dossier, but the mechanism can discard distinct content elsewhere.

The currently included June 30 10-Q also receives a `2026-Q3` period contribution from its July publication date. Filing date and reporting period are different facts. No actual period-collision loss was demonstrated in this dossier; this is a verified weakness in the identity mechanism, not an observed loss of a second filing here.

### Registry eligibility is not a judgment of every publisher's actual quality

None of the seven independent leads has an eligible counter-analysis role under the current registry. Some also lack a company match under the allowed URL rules. The system is correct to avoid making titles or inclusion into proof. However, the presence of relevant risk passages in Hated Moats, FSM, and Capital Blueprint demonstrates that missing automatic eligibility must not be presented as absence of useful independent material.

The opposite error would be to treat every risk-bearing page as equally sound. EarningsMoat's debt assertion contradicts the official [2025 annual report, Debt note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000007/now-20251231.htm). Capital Blueprint's cRPO figure contradicts the official [Q1 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000054/erq1fy26.htm). StockSentinel's acquisition timing conflicts with the closing already described in the same official Q1 release. The audit verifies these specific discrepancies, not every financial assertion in those articles.

## What should change in the proposed work

Prioritize the quality of classification, ordering, document identity, and exclusion explanations before introducing more search capacity. Existing adverse source leads should be assessed before spending on additional discovery. Preserve the automatic-eligibility boundary and distinguish useful but unqualified candidates from absence of candidates. Keep the current-terms limitation visible until an appropriately authoritative and current document is available.

Any change to publisher capabilities, source recommendation semantics, period identity, or documentary verification requires the normal explicit decision, documentation, and RED/GREEN implementation process. This audit authorizes none of those changes and does not ratify a three-query or twelve-source extension. That numerical proposal remains undecided.
