# Project Context

## Why This Project Exists

This prototype is part of an unsolicited application to join Decoding Discontinuity, through a directly useful contribution to Raphaëlle d'Ornano and her analysts.

The purpose is to turn the public research problem behind her vision into a useful analysis tool. Its relevance and practical value should make the author's contribution evident, rather than organizing the experience around an evaluation of the author. The candidature is served by usefulness, not the breadth of AI tooling or a speculative SaaS business.

The application should be framed around the value brought to Raphaëlle and her team, not around what the author hopes to learn, a future fintech project, or access to business opportunities.

## Who It Is For

The primary audience is Raphaëlle d'Ornano as a potential user of the analytical contribution.

Analysts working around Decoding Discontinuity and D'Ornano + Co. are also intended users. The system should take responsibility for preparatory research so they can concentrate on economic interpretation and judgment. Its usefulness and any acceleration require demonstration; it does not reproduce their proprietary methodology.

## What Decoding Discontinuity Means

Decoding Discontinuity is Raphaëlle d'Ornano's public body of research and analytical frameworks for understanding how generative and agentic AI can create structural breaks in companies, markets, and value capture. It is not simply a newsletter about AI adoption. Its central question is whether the analytical tools, business models, and competitive advantages inherited from the previous technological regime remain valid when machines become economic actors.

Raphaëlle distinguishes disruption from discontinuity:

- disruption changes the slope of an existing curve while preserving the fundamentals that produced it;
- discontinuity stops the old curve and creates a new one whose economics, competitive rules, and possible outcomes may be materially different.

In this thesis, generative AI is a discontinuity because it does more than make existing software or workers incrementally more productive. Agentic systems can receive an objective, use tools, coordinate actions across systems, and produce a business outcome. The unit of action can therefore move from a human operating software to software acting on a human's intent. That change can affect what companies sell, how they price it, which budgets they address, and where defensibility resides.

Raphaëlle's Orchestration Economics argues that, as intelligence becomes more abundant, value migrates toward the entity that controls the relationship between intent and outcome for a specific workflow. A durable position depends on more than possessing data or adding AI features. It depends on factors such as:

- proximity to the user's intent;
- access to compounding operational context, not merely raw data;
- control of the workflow and the permissions required to act;
- the ability to coordinate tools, systems, agents, and humans into a business outcome;
- the difficulty of bypassing, replacing, or reducing the company to an interchangeable system of record or API.

This vocabulary requires an important distinction:

- the **technical harness** makes model intelligence operational through context retrieval, tools, memory, state, delegation, evaluation, and recovery;
- the **economic orchestration layer** controls the relationship between intent, operational context, workflow execution, and the resulting business outcome.

The prototype may use a sophisticated technical harness, but harness sophistication is not itself proof of a durable economic orchestration position. The workbench exists to help analysts gather and evaluate evidence about that economic position.

Architectural resilience is consequently not a generic measure of technical quality. It asks whether a company can retain or strengthen its role as value migrates, or whether agents and external orchestration layers could abstract its interface, weaken its pricing model, or relegate it to a replaceable component. Historical growth, margins, retention, and brand may remain relevant, but they are insufficient if the company's role in the future workflow changes fundamentally.

Decoding Discontinuity applies this perspective through public research, institutional company analysis, strategic frameworks, and an investment thesis. Its proprietary frameworks, including ARAF™ and Durable Growth Moat™, are not public specifications for this project to recreate.

## How The Prototype Relates To The Thesis

The prototype is an independent, evidence-first technical contribution to the public research problem described above. It does not claim to calculate ARAF™, reproduce Durable Growth Moat™, predict an investment outcome, or automate Raphaëlle's judgment.

Its purpose is narrower and directly useful: situate a company, investigate an economically relevant activity or workflow, and prepare reasoned appraisals of the decisive questions using public evidence. The analyst should be able to understand where intent originates, who owns operational context, who controls execution, what could be abstracted, and how those observations may affect value capture. Findings remain attached to the investigated scope.

The product therefore demonstrates two different capabilities:

1. a technical harness that makes research reliable, inspectable, resumable, and efficient;
2. an analyst workbench that uses the resulting evidence to support human judgment about economic orchestration and architectural resilience.

The first capability is what the author builds. The second is the value offered to Raphaëlle and her analysts.

## What It Must Demonstrate

The application must demonstrate that public information can be transformed into an evidence-backed architectural assessment that concretely accelerates analysts' preparation work.

More specifically, it must show that the system can:

- investigate a company across financial, product, technical, and commercial sources;
- turn that research into a traceable and reproducible analytical process;
- identify context ownership, workflow control, systems of action, economic orchestration position, model dependencies, and abstraction risks;
- connect technical architecture signals to their possible economic consequences;
- distinguish facts, inferences, hypotheses, contradictions, and unresolved questions;
- let the analyst inspect, challenge, and override every proposed conclusion;
- prepare inspectable appraisals and connect retained judgments into an economic mechanism diagram with a fully reasoned note, or explain why no defensible synthesis can be established.

The intended first-minute conclusion is:

> “This tool could help our analysts prepare a rigorous architectural assessment faster, while keeping the evidence and judgment under their control.”

The prototype does not encode or claim to reproduce Decoding Discontinuity's proprietary methodology. It demonstrates a technical extension of the publicly observable research problem.

## What The Prototype Does

The prototype helps prepare an architectural assessment of a US-listed technology company from public sources.

The analyst supplies an intention and can refine a proposed framing. The system then prepares shared documentary material, investigates the framed questions and proposes reasoned appraisals for grouped human review. Retained judgments support an autonomous synthesis of the investigated scope. The current product and technical references own the precise workflow and its transition from the earlier corpus-approval model.

The evidence matrix is the inspectable foundation. For a supported synthesis, the economic mechanism diagram is the reference result and the note develops its argumentation. A justified absence of support remains a useful finding rather than a reason to force a diagram.

The strongest moment in the demonstration should not be “the AI produced an answer.” It should be:

> “Here is how each conclusion was built, challenged, and validated.”

## Definition Of Success

Within the first minute, Raphaëlle should recognize a tool relevant to her research: it makes an economic interpretation understandable, exposes its supporting evidence and objections, and prepares useful work without requiring her to coordinate internal execution.

The prototype succeeds if it produces a credible, inspectable research dossier faster than manual preparation while preserving uncertainty and human control.

It fails if it produces generic cited narrative, obscures uncertainty or economic meaning, makes the analyst manage routine research, or substitutes infrastructure display for useful analysis.

## Prototype Mindset

This is a narrow but deep candidature prototype, not a production SaaS platform.

- Analytical quality matters more than breadth of features.
- The user experience should feel like a serious research workbench, not a chatbot.
- Workflow execution should be legible through real persisted stages and progress, without exposing chain-of-thought or staging theatrical agent conversations.
- A curated completed dossier may be shown alongside a genuinely live discovery run, but cached work must never be presented as live.
- Visitor limits, retries, provider limits, and spending guards exist to prevent accidental abuse; they should not become a miniature enterprise or FinOps system.
- Technical decisions should favor speed, clarity, and a convincing demonstration unless a stronger mechanism is essential to evidence integrity.

Every implementation choice should therefore pass a simple test:

> Does this materially help Raphaëlle and her analysts understand, examine, or challenge the economic position under investigation?

If not, it should not delay the candidature prototype.
