## Users

Raphaëlle d'Ornano and analysts working around Decoding Discontinuity and D'Ornano + Co. need to examine how generative and agentic AI changes companies' economic positions. The prototype is a useful contribution in an unsolicited application to join Decoding Discontinuity. The analyst supplies an intention, can inspect and orient the preparation, and retains final authority over analytical judgment. The system takes responsibility for routine collection, reading, confrontation and preparation rather than asking the analyst to coordinate successive internal operations.

## Product Purpose

Prepare an inspectable analysis dossier that situates a US-listed technology company, investigates one economically relevant activity or workflow, confronts a precise question with evidence and competing interpretations, and identifies what deserves further investigation. Choose the scope with qualitative source-based justification; do not reconstruct its share of company revenue or generalize the finding to every activity.

| Principal capability | Useful result |
| --- | --- |
| Understand where value resides | A situated activity, its participants, dependencies, and the distinction between what the company supplies, recommends, executes and controls. |
| Examine resilience to the agentic transition | Interpretations of what agents could strengthen, weaken, bypass or redistribute, confronted with technical, commercial and financial evidence. |
| Prepare an actionable assessment | Reasoned appraisals by decisive question, their support, objections and unknowns, followed by a coherent synthesis where the retained base permits it. |

The shared evidence inventory and its matrix are the inspectable analytical foundation. For a supported synthesis, the economic mechanism diagram is the reference result; a derived, fully reasoned note develops the same interpretation. Success is useful economic understanding with traceable evidence and less analyst coordination. Measure acceleration and usefulness before claiming them.

## Positioning

Connect technical architecture to economic consequences: who receives intent, owns operational context, directs workflows, can act across systems and may retain or lose value as agents change the unit of execution. A technical harness makes intelligence operational through tools, context, memory, state, routing, evaluation and recovery. Its sophistication does not establish the company's economic orchestration position.

This is an independent contribution to a public research problem, not a reproduction of ARAF™, Durable Growth Moat™ or another proprietary methodology. It is neither an autonomous investment analyst nor a generic chat application. Economic interpretation, evidence, uncertainty and human judgment remain more important than infrastructure breadth or fluent narrative.

## Operating Context

### Company and research direction

The analyst searches by partial name, ticker, CIK or SEC URL and explicitly selects the company. Preserve the populated composer, optional angle and public HTTPS references through preflight and confirmation. Website enrichment improves recognition only; it cannot change legal identity or block a valid selection. References are research inputs, not evidence merely because the server received them. The analyst can choose a prepared model and supported reasoning effort before company confirmation; that choice governs the initial framing response and remains selected in the dossier chat until changed. Restoring the pre-confirmation composer draft after reload remains outside the MVP.

Preserve the existing preflight interaction: explicit Continue opens the in-place company confirmation with legal entity, ticker, exchange and SEC identity. Do not add numbered steps or explanatory headings where the controls already communicate the action. The later framing proposal does not authorize replacing that established interaction.

Company confirmation after admission creates a persistent dossier in framing. The same dossier identity connects the conversation, framing, research and result; creating it does not start research or consume an analysis.

A launch mandate authorizes bounded preparation under explicit source-selection rules, resource allowances and contextual-intervention conditions. Workbench proposes an activity scope and a useful set of research questions from a common foundation plus case-specific needs. Request contextual clarification only when missing information could materially change the research scope; always offer a system-proposed scope. Present the analyst’s request, any supplied clarification and the system’s proposed research program distinctly. Refinements are optional; the analyst is not required to write the research plan or select an investigative angle for every economic question. The common foundation examines the company’s position in the workflow, operational context and dependencies that are difficult to reproduce, substitution or bypass paths, and consequences for value capture. Workbench turns these themes into case-specific questions rather than imposing four fixed questions. The policy for material later scope changes remains design work.

Conversation and direct controls update the same visible framing draft. A clear refinement updates that draft and explains the changes without a separate acceptance for each edit. Preserve explicit analyst adjustments and prevent stale output from overwriting newer edits. Explicit `Start research` fixes the current framing, consumes one analysis and starts Sources. Keeping the proposal unchanged still requires this action. Framing assistance is authorized, bounded and accounted before launch; starting research preserves its consumption history. Abandoning framing consumes no analysis, and pausing or resuming launched research consumes no additional analysis. Source preparation and question investigations do not run in the background while framing remains open. The fixed ten-dimension questionnaire, uniform scoring and compulsory three-year direction are not the target assessment model.

Search and preflight are available before workspace admission. Non-billable preflight means no dossier or analysis reservation, not a promise of zero provider-credit use. Confirmation or direct entry to a protected workspace requests the reusable four-digit access code through the existing top drawer. Durable admission preserves access to the visitor's dossiers, role, quota and decisions across sessions. Dossiers and administration require the appropriate active access. Human research dossiers are separate from technical evaluations and browser validation. Benchmarks remain inspectable only in protected administration, including through direct links. The prototype reserves three analyst accounts for human research and one for local browser validation; validation data is explicitly labelled and never consumes or restores human research capacity.

### Workbench conversation and research control

Workbench is the analyst's conversational teammate throughout framing, preparation, Investigation and Synthesis. The analyst can question it about the dossier, request explanations or comparisons, explore an interpretation and orient permitted work without coordinating the specialist missions. Workbench brings together the relevant framing, source material, assertions, appraisals, decisions, results and limitations, with inspectable references to available support. It coordinates authorized requests through the responsible research missions and explains their outcomes. Conversational hypotheses and explanations remain distinguishable from controlled dossier artifacts and analyst decisions.

The dossier retains one analyst-facing conversation. A Workbench turn handles an active request through the shared harness and may contain several model invocations and operations. The turn is distinct from the research duration: the analyst can converse while research missions continue. During an active turn, new messages queue by default for a following turn. Explicit `Steer` instead brings a message into the active turn so Workbench can adapt before finishing, preserving useful work. Distinguish queued, transmitted and processed work from an announced result.

Workbench may ask its model once to revise an explanation that conflicts with recorded dossier state or operations. The correction does not repeat actions or replace the model’s explanation with a canned answer. A separately enabled consistency control applies to final explanations after operations or changed authoritative dossier facts; unchanged tool-free discussion and deterministic confirmations remain exempt. It is not a general truth assessment. If the final explanation remains doubtful, display a separate reserve and its available consistency estimate. If the check is unavailable, say so without inventing a score. Preserve the original explanation, correction and assessment for authorized audit.

After launch, Workbench transmits authorized instructions within the fixed framing. Each transmitted request retains the original message and Workbench's interpretation, and receives an explicit result, limit or explained inability. Confirm transmission separately from completed treatment. Requests that would reopen the framing are signalled rather than applied; conversation cannot create another documentary complement phase. Framing, requests, decisions and artifacts retain their domain owners instead of being reconstructed from conversation text.

On an explicit analyst pause, suspend research progression until explicit `Resume`. An already-engaged operation may finish and its result is retained subject to remaining controls. Workbench remains available to discuss existing material and record instructions; their research execution waits for resumption. A steering message is not itself a research pause. Resumption preserves human prerequisites and earlier judgments. `Start research` and appraisal adoption remain explicit analyst decisions; Workbench prepares and explains them without making them for the analyst. When an interpretation or system stop calls for analyst consent, Workbench asks directly in the conversation (and on affected workspace views) as an interactive approval card (`Deny`, `Allow once`, `Always allow`, or the named choices for that stop). Choosing `Always allow` or `Always continue` grants that action automatically on the current dossier until the analyst revokes it from the composer's Commands menu (`Allowed actions`) or the card receipt (`Stop allowing`). Only an analyst can create or revoke a grant.

### Source preparation and corpus review

Sources collects material, reads and checks its contributions, and organizes usable material under the launch mandate. The system organizes preparation; its specialized agent conducts documentary inquiry across the captured corpus, choosing useful readings and confrontations. The output includes source identity, exact captures and passages, qualifications, permitted uses, selection reasons, gaps and an explicit preparation outcome. One shared documentary foundation serves the framed questions.

Preparation tracks shared documentary needs alongside sources. Each need identifies the information sought, can serve several framed questions or common context, and retains the work performed, usable contributions, documentary gaps and technically unfinished work separately. The system supplies this structure; the analyst does not have to write a documentary task list. Sources prepares material for the questions without deciding their economic answers.

Prioritize distinct useful contributions, relevant periods and genuinely independent producers rather than filling a source-count target. Unavailable reading or qualification does not prove irrelevance. Inclusion, reading availability, documentary coverage and factual support remain separate judgments, with useful imperfect material retaining explicit attribution and restrictions.

Integrate the initial availability check into planned discovery. Absence of an initial filing, investor-relations address or supplied reference does not itself require the analyst to justify continuing before those searches. A completed search with no result differs from unavailable discovery; a candidate differs from a successful reading, useful contribution or factual support. Preserve these distinctions through partial failures.

Delegated source selection is explainable and versioned. Retain every document with at least one controlled contribution useful to the dossier and permitted under the mandate; do not apply a second code-ranked or model-proposed shortlist merely to reduce the collection. This does not require acquiring or qualifying every candidate, remove resource ceilings or override analyst exclusions. Unread references and other candidates remain inspectable leads without becoming supporting material. Routine selection requires no separate human corpus-approval stop. Inclusion does not establish truth, erase restrictions or imply analyst endorsement. Important exceptions require contextual intervention; ordinary gaps can remain explicit while independent work continues. Operational thresholds remain to be specified before activation.

During preparation, Sources can request targeted discovery for an existing documentary need when useful material is absent from the available candidates and observed links. The system authorizes and executes that discovery within the active phase and allowance. Returned candidates do not themselves establish a reading or contribution. Initial targeted discovery remains initial work rather than consuming Investigation's later complement.

Reading preserves the representation actually consulted, reproducible passage references and actually observed link destinations with their origin. An index or guide label does not establish that linked material was read. A partial reading cannot establish absence from a whole document. Comparisons preserve differences in period, scope, units, definitions, assumptions and inconclusive outcomes, without discarding independent useful contributions because another use is restricted.

A technical execution interruption is not a documentary completion or a finding of insufficient evidence. Preserve the dossier, retained material and unfinished preparation; report the cause accurately and keep recovery within the remaining launch authorization. Repeated unusable model replies cannot by themselves close the dossier. When a technical stop cannot be resumed, including insufficient context capacity, keep preparation paused and offer `Use what's prepared` to hand back the retained material with its limits; do not offer an unavailable Resume action.

After Sources hands back a controlled partial result, the system may start Investigation on the usable material while retaining the unfinished documentary work and its consequences. Appraisals depending on that work cannot be presented as complete, and an unperformed search cannot become a finding of documentary insufficiency. Analyst pauses, missing authority and exhausted global resources still prevent progression. The system validates handoff; the agent's completion message does not authorize it. Reaching a preparation limit never ends the dossier as a failure: when Sources uses its time window while its counted capacity still admits work, research pauses so the analyst can choose `Use what's prepared` (hands back with limits), `Continue once` (renews the window once), or `Always continue` (renews the window and grants automatic continuation on future time limits for that dossier while capacity allows); when no capacity remains, the system hands back the material with its limits without asking, and states `No usable material prepared` if no qualified source was retained. [TECH](context/TECH.md#sources-mission-and-prepared-material) owns the execution and version contracts.

Investigation first examines the prepared material and groups missing information that could materially change its appraisals. When useful, Sources performs one autonomous bounded complement phase for these shared needs; it may contain several searches or retrievals. Initial preparation does not consume that phase merely to fill documentary roles. Remaining gaps do not automatically open another research cycle. Counts, costs and time limits are implementation decisions within the technical resource contract.

### Sources view

Research starts only through the Sources mission, never by falling back to another preparation path. The research program respects the number of questions documentary preparation can take on: the program card shows its count against that limit, and neither the analyst nor Workbench can add a question beyond it, while existing questions stay editable and removable. Start is offered only when it would be accepted; otherwise it is unavailable before any attempt, with the reason and the next step, such as removing questions or waiting for activation, and no analysis is consumed. Coverage presets `Balanced`, `First-party depth` and `Independent scrutiny` adjust bounded discovery priorities, not evidence rules.

The Sources section opens on the preparation: documentary needs against the framed questions they serve, each with an outcome that remains in progress until Sources hands off. How preparation ran stays one disclosure away. The source matrix lists every source with inclusion, reading, documentary contributions, control and restriction as separate facts, with search and filters restored by the URL. The reader shows cited excerpts with their neighbouring passages, never invented pages, the contributions in documentary terms, effective permitted use beside the control's reported use, observed links and the original document. Contributions are labelled asserted fact, attributed argument, forecast or context; they never present an inventory claim class.

A targeted check or selection challenge from the reader prepares a Workbench message about the source; the analyst edits and sends it, and Sources treats it through the instruction contract. Nothing is sent without the analyst.

### Evidence matrix and analytical review

A shared company inventory retains useful context and atomic sourced assertions across the dossier. It need not be exhaustive before useful appraisals can be prepared. Sources supplies qualified documentary material; Investigation enriches the inventory through controlled contributions and reuses the same assertion with distinct question-specific rationales.

| Claim class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Fact` | An assertion explicitly established by cited evidence. |
| `Inference` | A reasoned conclusion derived from cited facts, with its reasoning exposed. |
| `Hypothesis` | A plausible proposition requiring investigation. |
| `Unknown` | A material question not established by the examined evidence. |

Assertions and their intended uses receive mandatory controls when contributed, before reuse. Documentary integrity, admissible analytical use and human adoption of an interpretation are distinct. Routine human confirmation of every assertion and link is not a prerequisite for preparing appraisals. Failed integrity cannot be waived by an analyst decision. Limitations must restrict the affected use or remain explicit, rather than silently becoming successful validation.

The evidence matrix makes assertions, question uses, passages, supporting and opposing relations, unknowns, controls and corrections inspectable. Linked assertions, including `Unknown`, establish evidence presence only. A separate server-owned question-outcome projection distinguishes evidence presence, the controlled analytical result and the analyst's review decision; clients do not derive answer status from claim counts. Answer labels and dependent transitions remain blocked until that projection is implemented and verified. Useful company context need not have a question link. Correction and enrichment preserve earlier versions and identify affected uses; exact identity and freshness presentation remain to be specified.

Retained content, search candidates, model context and analytical passages are different artifacts. Missing or corrupt content cannot be silently replaced. Required references must reach the intended analytical use or fail explicitly. Saved-source search preserves exact quotations and provenance without itself creating assertions or granting factual authority; unsupported captures expose their reading limitation.

### Assessment and review

Investigation conducts one common inquiry across the framed questions. It tests alternative interpretations, connects observations to possible economic mechanisms, examines objections and decisive gaps, and proposes a distinct reasoned appraisal for each question. It can conclude justified insufficiency after investigation; this completes preparation for the question but is not an answer or a retained appraisal. Unperformed work, a required revision or unavailable technical capability remains incomplete and cannot be presented as that analytical outcome.

Each appraisal exposes its scope, interpretation, support, assumptions, objections, unknowns and confidence explanation together. Confidence depends on inspectable evidence and its limitations, never model self-confidence. An explicit horizon is relevant to future claims; no replacement categorical confidence scale or uniform horizon is selected. A controlled substantive appraisal makes a proposed answer available for review; only the exact version retained by the analyst becomes a retained answer and eligible Synthesis input.

The analyst reviews Investigation output as a group, with the included appraisal versions identifiable. Review becomes available only after every assigned question has either a controlled substantive appraisal or controlled justified insufficiency. The analyst can retain reviewed appraisals in one action, correct an appraisal or leave a question for follow-up. Unreviewed proposals and justified-insufficiency results cannot become retained appraisals through a default or later generation. Decisions remain audited and tied to the version examined. If no substantive appraisal is retained, stay at appraisal review with an explanation and a way to continue; do not start Synthesis or call this documentary insufficiency.

### Derived views and workspace

After grouped appraisal review, Synthesis autonomously connects retained appraisals into an economic interpretation. It can propose new relationships and transversal inferences, explaining how the inputs support them. An arrow is an analytical statement, not a connection created because two concepts appear in the dossier. Actors and workflow anchor the mechanism without becoming an unsupported claim of complete internal architecture.

For a supported interpretation, the reference mechanism diagram and derived reasoned note are submitted and controlled together. The overview exposes relevant conditions and uncertainty; relationship details connect the explanation to appraisals, passages, objections and limitations. The note develops the same interpretation at greater depth. Retaining input appraisals does not validate new synthesis relationships automatically.

When retained, instructed appraisals support no defensible mechanism, return a reasoned non-establishment finding with its examined basis, limits and missing information instead of forcing a diagram. No retained appraisals, pending human judgment, technical interruption and justified non-establishment remain distinct. A result becoming available does not imply human adoption of the synthesis. There is no routine final synthesis-approval checkpoint.

Synthesis can request bounded analytical re-examination of an appraisal or a necessary uninstructed premise found in already available material. Investigation examines its inventory contribution and appraisal effects; Synthesis cannot directly establish that new premise. This return does not open another documentary phase. A material proposed change to a retained appraisal returns as a targeted human exception. Independent work continues. A limited diagram and note can be delivered together if the unaffected base supports a coherent, useful reduced scope; delivery waits if the overall reading depends on the contested premise. Earlier decisions and adverse controls remain visible with the explanation of their resolution.

The workspace centers actual progress and a meaningful timeline, with contextual interventions and optional dedicated Sources, Investigation, inventory and Synthesis detail views. The composer becomes compact and floats at the bottom center after launch, available for orientation and follow-up. Work cards remain inactive until actual execution starts, then activate with animation. A question does not automatically create its own source matrix. Completed-result composition remains design work. The adopted shell uses Progress, Sources, Investigation and Synthesis, with Questions, Hypotheses, Requests and Review under Investigation, and Mechanism and Full brief under Synthesis. Inventory opens over the current screen from a persistent control at the end of the workspace section bar (changed on 2026-11-17 from a floating bottom-right dock that covered content); it shows the actual assertion total without a class distribution. Wide Progress layouts place Sources and Investigation to the left of persisted progress and Synthesis to its right; narrow layouts stack those regions. The interface-only transition preserves installed checkpoints and makes unavailable missions explicit.

Use Agent Question for contextual clarification within the framing conversation; persistence, branching and authority belong to the application. Preserve source readers, reference inputs, useful inspection affordances and loading geometry when evolving the interface. A visual preview is labelled `Preview — for visual reference only`, separate from documentary captures and analytical input; unavailable preview retains access to the original and source details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sources are selected under an explicit bounded mandate. Rejected or inadmissible material cannot support downstream outputs; selection alone never establishes factual authority.
- Reuse exact, versioned evidence with atomic assertions and explicit intended uses. Attribution, uncertainty, opposing evidence and restrictions survive every handoff.
- Admission, appraisal and synthesis controls examine their own contributions. Prior valid controls can be reused without repeating all earlier work; a new interpretation still needs its own justification.
- Grouped appraisal review records identifiable human judgments before Synthesis. Later revisions, retries or favorable controls cannot overwrite those judgments or erase earlier objections.
- Progress reflects persisted work. Retry, continuation and recovery do not duplicate evidence, reservations or external effects, reset cumulative consumption, bypass a human prerequisite or revive cancelled work.
- Every assigned need and engaged check has an explicit outcome. Useful partial work, justified insufficiency, unfinished checks, pending judgment and reasoned synthesis non-establishment remain distinguishable. Missing configuration or model capability is not an analytical finding.
- Final availability follows controlled mission artifacts and review prerequisites, not completion of ten dimension decisions, source counts, schema acceptance or an agent declaring success. It does not guarantee that every possible omission was discovered.

Sourced economic figures, ratios and projections retain definitions, period, attribution and limitations. The prototype produces no new financial calculations, reconstructs no activity revenue share and performs no dedicated financial collection or quantitative modeling.

The MVP includes durable private admission, company resolution and framing, bounded shared documentary preparation, common Investigation, a reusable evidence inventory, grouped appraisal review, autonomous Synthesis, provider-independent execution, one curated case and methodology with measured limits. Administration supports inspection, audited quota restoration, audited dossier withdrawal that releases the dossier's analysis once, and stopping new paid work, rather than a general policy configuration console.

Excluded without a new decision: investment recommendations or trading, aggregate resilience scoring, non-US markets, continuous monitoring, simultaneous analyst collaboration, local file/PDF upload, pasted text without public provenance, fine-tuning, general crawling, autonomous publication, proprietary-methodology reproduction, chat as the primary interface, cross-dossier learning and institutional memory. Automatic translation remains deferred until the English MVP is complete. No second workflow or persistence authority is selected.

## MVP Acceptance

The intended admitted analyst must be able to:

1. Select and confirm a supported company with the angle and public references preserved, open its persistent framing dossier after admission, and receive a proposed framing under an explicit mandate.
2. Optionally refine the same proposal through conversation or direct controls, then use `Start research` to fix it and consume one analysis; source preparation and Investigation begin only after this event.
3. Observe actual shared preparation and Investigation progress, inspect useful material and limitations optionally, and handle material exceptions without coordinating internal operations. Ask Workbench to explain or connect dossier material and results, explore an interpretation and orient permitted work. Queue messages or steer Workbench during a turn, follow transmitted requests, and explicitly pause or resume research while retaining the conversation.
4. Examine distinct appraisals of the framed questions through shared assertions, exact passages, control outcomes, counter-evidence and decisive unknowns. Inspect the effect of the single bounded grouped documentary complement when used.
5. Retain identifiable reviewed appraisals in a grouped action, correct or defer a question with history preserved, and remain at review if nothing is retained.
6. Inspect the autonomous reference diagram and argued note from the retained base, or the justified non-establishment finding. Trace a relationship to its inputs and distinguish limited delivery from waiting on a central contested premise.
7. Reconnect to the preserved dossier, conversation, scope, evidence, decisions and progress. Inspect methodology and measured limits; an administrator can inspect operations, restore research quota with a reason, withdraw a research dossier to release its analysis with a reason and stop new paid work.
